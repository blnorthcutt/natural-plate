The Natural Plate

A skill, not a rulebook.

Plan your week with whole-food plant-based recipes that make natural eating simple, seasonal, and delicious. Browse recipes, build your meal plan, and generate a grocery list — all in one place.

How it works

Browse Recipes

Explore a growing collection of plant-forward recipes organized by season, meal, difficulty, and dietary preference. Filter, search, and find your next favorite dish.

Plan Your Week

Build a 7-day meal plan with breakfast, lunch, snack, and dinner slots. Assign recipes from the bank, save named plans, and come back to them anytime.

Smart Grocery List

Generate a consolidated grocery list from any meal plan. Ingredients are grouped by category — produce, grains, legumes, pantry, and more — so you shop faster.

Built on the Natural Eating framework

The Natural Plate brings the Natural Eating guide to life. Every recipe follows the natural plate proportions — half vegetables, a quarter whole grains, a quarter legumes, plus a handful of nuts and seeds for healthy fats.

No counting. No restriction. Just whole foods, seasonal ingredients, and meals you'll genuinely look forward to.

Five principles:

  • Whole — foods close to nature
  • Seasonal — eat with the rhythms of your place
  • Conditional — adjust to how you feel
  • Balanced — variety across the week
  • Mindful — eat with attention and gratitude
