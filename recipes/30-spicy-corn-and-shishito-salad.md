## Description

This no-cook corn salad from Lidey Heuck is summer in a bowl: raw sweet corn kernels tossed with blistered shishito peppers and a punchy cumin-lime vinaigrette. The peppers are quickly sautéed until tender and beginning to brown, then stirred into the raw corn along with diced red onion, garlic, and jalapeño for heat. A shower of Cotija or feta (optional) and fresh cilantro finishes the salad, which comes together in 15 minutes flat. The raw corn stays crisp and juicy — a refreshing counterpoint to the smoky, charred peppers — and the dressing mellows the bite of the onion and garlic as they sit. Reader comments suggest leaving shishitos whole for a blistered-pepper effect, adding diced avocado for creaminess, or swapping in grilled corn for extra depth.

## Instructions

1. **Make the dressing.** In a small bowl, whisk 2 tablespoons olive oil with the lime juice, cumin, and ¾ teaspoon salt. Stir in the diced red onion and minced garlic and set aside until ready to use. (Do this step first so the onions and garlic have time to mellow slightly in the dressing.)

2. **Sauté the peppers.** Place the corn kernels in a large bowl and set aside. In a medium (10-inch) sauté pan, heat the remaining 1½ tablespoons olive oil over medium heat. Add the sliced shishitos, diced jalapeño, and a pinch of salt and cook, stirring occasionally, until the peppers are tender and beginning to brown, 4 to 6 minutes.

3. **Toss and serve.** Add the hot peppers and the dressing to the bowl with the corn and toss well. Add the cheese, if using, and toss again. Garnish with cilantro and serve immediately.

## Notes

- **Storage:** Best eaten the day it's made — the raw corn stays crisp and the peppers retain their just-sautéed texture. Leftovers keep in the fridge for up to a day but the corn will soften.
- **Make-ahead:** The dressing (with onion and garlic) can be made up to a day ahead and refrigerated. Sauté the peppers just before serving.
- **Variations:** Leave the shishitos whole and blister them in the oil for a dramatic, restaurant-style presentation (a reader favorite). Add diced avocado for creaminess. For a smokier flavor, grill the corn and cut the kernels off the cob instead of using raw kernels — several commenters recommend this. Swap Cotija for crumbled feta, queso fresco, or omit the cheese entirely for a vegan version. Use a mix of raw and grilled corn for textural contrast. Substitute white wine vinegar for some of the oil in the dressing.
- **Serving suggestion:** Serve as a light summer dinner with crusty bread, or as a vibrant side for grilled chicken, fish, or tacos. Pairs beautifully with a cold beer or a crisp white wine.
