## Description

Crunchy cucumbers and creamy avocados are the stars of this simple five-ingredient salad from Ali Slagle. Peeling the cucumbers in alternating stripes helps them soak up seasonings while maintaining their shape. After being salted and drained, they're tossed with ripe avocado that breaks down just enough to create a glossy, creamy coating — no oil needed. A hit of red-pepper flakes adds heat, and the whole thing comes together in 15 minutes. It's the perfect no-cook meal for hot summer days, endlessly adaptable with herbs, lettuces, beans, or whatever you have on hand.

## Instructions

1. **Crisp the scallions.** Transfer the sliced scallions to a small bowl of ice water and set aside to crisp while you prep the rest.

2. **Prep the cucumbers.** Peel the cucumbers in alternating strips (peel one strip lengthwise, skip the next, peel the next — leaving a striped pattern). Trim the ends. If desired, halve lengthwise and scoop out the seeds. Cut into bite-size pieces — thin slices, ½-inch cubes, or smashed into irregular shapes. Transfer to a colander set in the sink, toss with 1 teaspoon salt, and let drain at least 5 minutes or up to 15.

3. **Cube the avocado.** When you're ready to eat, halve the avocados and remove the pits. Scoop the flesh from the skins with a spoon and cut into ½-inch cubes. Transfer to a large bowl, add the lemon juice, and season with salt. Stir gently to combine — the acid prevents browning.

4. **Combine.** Shake the cucumbers in the colander to shed excess moisture, then transfer to the bowl with the avocado. Drain the scallions, shake off the water, and add them to the bowl.

5. **Finish and serve.** Stir the salad vigorously just until the avocado begins to break down and coats the cucumbers in a glossy layer — the majority of the avocado pieces should still remain cubed. Season to taste with more salt, lemon juice, and red-pepper flakes or hot sauce. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- **Storage:** This salad is best eaten immediately — the avocado will brown and the cucumbers will weep if left to sit. If you must make ahead, prep the components separately (salt the cucumbers, cube the avocado and toss it with lemon juice) and combine just before serving.
- **Variations:** The recipe is endlessly adaptable. Add a handful of fresh mint or cilantro, toss in a cup of cooked chickpeas or white beans, crumble in some feta, add soft-boiled eggs, or top with toasted nuts. Many commenters recommend swapping the red-pepper flakes for chili crisp or gochugaru for a different kind of heat. Lime juice works beautifully in place of lemon.
- **Serving suggestion:** Serve on its own as a light lunch, or as a bright side dish alongside grilled tofu, grain bowls, or tacos. For a heartier meal, serve over a bed of baby spinach or arugula dressed with a splash of rice vinegar.
- **Alternating stripes explained:** Run your peeler from top to bottom in one straight line, skip a finger's width, then peel the next strip. Continue around the cucumber until you have alternating peeled and unpeeled stripes — this helps the seasoning stick while the skin keeps the pieces from falling apart.
