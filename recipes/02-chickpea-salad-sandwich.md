## Description

A bright, protein-packed lunch that comes together in 15 minutes with no cooking. Mashed chickpeas replace the tuna in this classic salad — creamy from the yogurt, briny from the capers, and crunchy from the celery. It is the lunch you will actually look forward to.

## Instructions

1. **Mash the chickpeas.** Drain and rinse the chickpeas. Add them to a bowl and mash with a fork until broken down but still chunky — you want texture, not paste.
2. **Prep the vegetables.** Dice the celery into small pieces. Finely dice the red onion. If the onion is strong, soak the diced pieces in cold water for 5 minutes, then drain.
3. **Mix the salad.** Add the yogurt, capers, lemon juice, olive oil, celery, and red onion to the mashed chickpeas. Stir to combine. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
4. **Assemble.** Toast the bread if desired. Lay lettuce on 4 slices, pile on the chickpea salad, and top with the remaining bread.

## Notes

- **Storage:** The chickpea salad keeps in the fridge for up to 5 days. Assemble sandwiches fresh.
- **Variations:** For a dairy-free version, use tahini or hummus instead of yogurt. Add chopped fresh dill or parsley. Swap in chopped bell pepper for celery.
- **Packing tip:** This travels well in a lunchbox — pack the salad and bread separately and assemble at lunchtime to avoid soggy bread.
