## Description

A hearty, warming chili that is the definition of batch-cook comfort. Lentils and sweet potatoes simmer together in a smoky, spiced tomato broth until tender and thick. It freezes beautifully, tastes even better the next day, and requires nothing more than a side of bread or a simple salad to make a complete meal.

## Instructions

1. **Sauté the aromatics.** Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the diced onion and cook for 5 minutes until translucent. Add the garlic and cook for 1 more minute.
2. **Bloom the spices.** Add the chili powder, cumin, and smoked paprika. Stir for 30 seconds until fragrant.
3. **Add the main ingredients.** Add the rinsed lentils, peeled and diced sweet potato (½-inch cubes), crushed tomatoes, and vegetable broth. Stir to combine.
4. **Simmer.** Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low. Cover and simmer for 30–35 minutes, until the lentils are tender and the sweet potato is soft.
5. **Finish and serve.** Taste and adjust salt. Squeeze in the lime juice. Serve in bowls topped with fresh cilantro and any optional toppings.

## Notes

- **Storage:** Keeps in the fridge for up to 6 days. Freezes perfectly for up to 3 months — portion into containers for easy meals.
- **Variations:** Use brown lentils or red lentils instead of green. Add a diced bell pepper with the onion. For extra heat, add a minced chipotle in adobo with the spices.
- **Serving suggestion:** Serve with a slice of whole-grain bread, over brown rice, or with a simple side salad.
