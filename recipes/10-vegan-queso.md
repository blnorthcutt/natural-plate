## Description

Thanks to a hefty dose of funky nutritional yeast and smoky heat from paprika and chipotle, this cashew-based dip has enough nacho-cheesy vibes to please all kinds of eaters. Unlike traditional queso, it's made from whole, plant-based ingredients — sweet potato and cashews give it body and creaminess, while a blender does all the work. Serve warm with tortilla chips, over nachos, or as a sauce for mac and cheese.

## Instructions

1. **Bloom the garlic and spices.** Combine the oil and garlic in a medium saucepan and cook over medium-high heat, stirring frequently, until the garlic is fragrant and barely golden, about 2 minutes. Add the paprika and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
2. **Simmer the base.** Add 3 cups water, the sweet potato, cashews, nutritional yeast, chipotle, adobo, mustard, and MSG (if using). Gently simmer, stirring occasionally, until the sweet potato and cashews are tender, 15 to 20 minutes.
3. **Blend until silky.** Carefully transfer the mixture to a blender and puree until completely smooth. Generously season with salt to taste. It should be cheesy, slightly spicy, and have a mellow sweetness. If it's too sharp, spicy, or bland, it needs more salt. If too thick, add water a tablespoon at a time.
4. **Serve.** Serve warm or at room temperature with tortilla chips, steamed broccoli, or French fries.

## Notes

- **Storage:** Store refrigerated for up to 1 week. Reheat gently, adding a splash of water if needed.
- **Variations:** Add a can of green chiles or pickled jalapeño brine for extra tang. A spoonful of white miso adds deeper umami. Use it as a sauce for vegan mac and cheese — it makes enough for about 2 boxes of pasta.
- **Serving suggestion:** The classic vegan nacho companion — make it alongside the Loaded Vegan Nachos recipe.
