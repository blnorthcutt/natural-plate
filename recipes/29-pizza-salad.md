## Description

For pizza in salad form, this clever recipe from Sohla El-Waylly tosses a crisp and briny mix of romaine lettuce, pepperoncini, black olives, and red onion in a bright, grated tomato vinaigrette. Spicy pepperoni strips are sizzled until crisp, then their rendered fat is used to toast panko bread crumbs spiked with oregano, garlic, and red-pepper flakes — a crunchy, salty topping that delivers all the flavor of a New York slice in every bite. Tear fresh mozzarella over the top and dinner is ready in 35 minutes. The pepperoni bread crumbs are so good you'll want to make extra to keep on hand for pasta, eggs, roasted vegetables, and anything else that could use a trip to pizza town.

## Instructions

1. **Render the pepperoni.** In a medium nonstick or well-seasoned cast-iron skillet, add 2 tablespoons olive oil and the pepperoni strips and place over medium heat. Cook, stirring frequently, until the pepperoni is rendered and crisp, about 5 minutes. Remove from heat and use a slotted spoon to transfer the pepperoni to a plate.

2. **Toast the bread crumbs.** Return the skillet with the rendered fat to medium-low heat. Add the panko bread crumbs and cook, stirring frequently, until browned and toasty, about 5 minutes. Remove from heat. Stir in the garlic powder, then the oregano, crumbling it between your fingers as you sprinkle it over the mixture. Add the crispy pepperoni bits, scraping in any fat clinging to the plate, and stir until evenly combined. Season with salt and red-pepper flakes to taste.

3. **Make the dressing and assemble the salad.** On the large holes of a box grater, grate the tomato into a large bowl, discarding the remaining skin. Whisk in the pepperoncini brine and the remaining 2 tablespoons olive oil. Add salt, sugar (if the tomato needs it), and more brine to taste. Add the romaine, pepperoncini, olives, and red onion; toss to combine.

4. **Serve.** Divide the salad among plates, pouring over any dressing remaining in the bowl. Tear the mozzarella into pieces and divide over the salad. Top with the pepperoni bread crumbs and serve right away.

## Notes

- **Pepperoni tip:** If using turkey pepperoni, increase the olive oil used to cook it to 3 tablespoons. If using a plant-based substitute, increase the initial oil to 4 tablespoons — plant-based pepperoni renders less fat.
- **Make-ahead:** Make a big batch of the pepperoni bread crumbs and store them in the fridge for up to a week. They're fantastic on pasta, eggs, roasted vegetables, and more.
- **Variations:** Swap the fresh mozzarella for burrata for an even creamier finish. Add a splash of red wine vinegar to the tomato dressing for extra brightness. For a more authentic pizza flavor, stir a little grated Parmesan into the bread crumbs. If you don't have a beefsteak tomato, macerate and lightly crush mini plum tomatoes instead of grating.
- **Serving suggestion:** This salad is best served immediately after assembling — the romaine stays crisp and the bread crumbs stay crunchy. Pairs beautifully with a glass of red wine or a cold beer.
