## Description

A salty-sweet summertime pie: a crisp, buttery pretzel crust filled with a cloud-light whipped cream cheese mousse and piled high with glossy sugared strawberries. Adapted from the NYT Cooking original, with one key change — the heavy cream is whipped separately to stiff peaks and folded in, which makes the filling set up perfectly for clean slices every time. No oven fuss, no gelatin, just chilling. A stunning dessert for a party or a warm-weather treat.

## Instructions

1. **Heat the oven.** Heat the oven to 400°F/200°C. Grease the inside of a 9-inch deep pie dish (or a 9x13 baking dish) with butter.
2. **Make the crust.** In a stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment, or in a medium bowl with a handheld electric mixer, beat the 14 tablespoons butter, ½ cup of the confectioners' sugar, the ground pretzels, flour, baking soda and salt on low until just combined, then increase to high and beat until you get a soft, cohesive dough. Press the dough evenly across the bottom and up the sides of the dish. Bake until slightly puffed and deep golden brown, 12 to 15 minutes. Cool completely before filling.
3. **Make the filling base.** In a stand mixer with the paddle attachment, or in a large bowl with a handheld mixer, beat the cream cheese on high for 1 minute to soften. Add 1½ cups of the confectioners' sugar and beat on low until combined. Scrape down the sides, then beat on high until fluffy, about 2 minutes.
4. **Whip and fold the cream.** In a separate bowl, whip the very cold heavy cream to stiff peaks. (Whip it separately from the cream cheese mixture and fold it in — the filling sets up far better for slicing than adding it in a splash at a time.) Fold the whipped cream into the cream cheese mixture in three additions, gently, until smooth and billowy. Spread evenly into the cooled crust.
5. **Chill.** Refrigerate at least 2 hours, or up to overnight, until the filling is firm enough to slice.
6. **Finish and serve.** Just before serving, toss the sliced strawberries with the remaining 2 tablespoons confectioners' sugar, pile them on top of the pie, and serve immediately.

## Notes

- **Storage:** The filled (but un-topped) pie keeps in the fridge for up to 2 days; top with strawberries only at serving time, or the berries will weep.
- **Make-ahead:** A great party dessert — bake the crust and make the filling up to a day ahead, chill overnight, then top with berries when you're ready to serve.
- **Variations:** Swap strawberries for raspberries, blueberries, or a mix, and add a little lime zest. No pretzels on hand? The filling works beautifully over a graham cracker crust or blind-baked pie crust. If you prefer a softer crust, bake the crust without the baking soda for 15-20 minutes like a shortbread.
- **Serving suggestion:** Cut with a sharp knife dipped in hot water and wiped dry between slices for the cleanest cuts.
