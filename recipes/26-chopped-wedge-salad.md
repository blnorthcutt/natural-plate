## Description

A clever rework of the classic steakhouse wedge: instead of serving a stubborn wedge that loses its toppings halfway through, everything is chopped to the same size and tossed together so the lettuce gets thoroughly dressed and every bite has the full picture — juicy tomatoes, smoky bacon, crunchy iceberg and creamy blue cheese. It comes together in 25 minutes and works as a meal on its own or as a showy side for burgers, steak or garlic bread. Adapted from the NYT Cooking recipe by Ali Slagle.

## Instructions

1. **Marinate the tomatoes and shallot.** In a large bowl, stir together the cherry tomatoes, shallot, lemon zest and juice, Worcestershire sauce and a pinch of salt. Set aside — this quick pickle softens the shallot and seasons the tomatoes while the bacon cooks.
2. **Make the dressing.** In a small bowl, stir together the buttermilk, yogurt and mayonnaise. Season to taste with salt and pepper. It should be thick but pourable — loosen with a splash more buttermilk if needed.
3. **Cook the bacon.** Place the bacon in a large nonstick or well-seasoned cast-iron skillet over medium-high. Once it starts sizzling, cook, stirring often, until golden, 4 to 6 minutes. Turn off the heat, scoop out the bacon with a slotted spoon or fish spatula and drain on a paper towel-lined plate. (Save the rendered fat for frying eggs or roasting potatoes.)
4. **Toss the salad.** To the bowl of tomatoes and shallot, add the iceberg lettuce, blue cheese, chives, crispy bacon and about three-quarters of the dressing. Toss to combine, then taste and adjust with more salt, pepper and dressing. Eat right away, while the lettuce is crunchy and the bacon is crisp.

## Notes

- **Storage:** Best eaten immediately — the lettuce wilts and the bacon softens as it sits. The dressing and the tomato-shallot mixture keep separately in the fridge for up to 3 days.
- **Make-ahead:** The dressing, cooked bacon and marinated tomatoes can all be prepped up to a day ahead; toss everything together just before serving.
- **Variations:** No buttermilk on hand? Make your own: stir 1 tablespoon lemon juice into 1 cup milk and let sit 5 minutes. Swap the bacon for sautéed walnuts or chickpeas fried in a little oil for a vegetarian version; a feta dressing works beautifully in place of the blue cheese. Many commenters add sautéed chickpeas or sliced chicken for extra bulk and protein.
- **Serving suggestion:** Serve as a main-course salad with crusty bread, or alongside burgers, steak, or garlic bread. Pair with steamed corn or roasted potatoes for a full steakhouse dinner.
