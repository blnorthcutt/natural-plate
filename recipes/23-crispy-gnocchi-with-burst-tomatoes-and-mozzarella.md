## Description

Pan-fried gnocchi is a faster, stovetop version of baked pasta: store-bought gnocchi browns right in the skillet — no boiling required — for crispy outsides and chewy middles. Here they're tossed with cherry tomatoes that burst into a bright sauce with browned butter, garlic and red-pepper flakes, then topped with fresh mozzarella and broiled until molten and blistered in spots. It's a 25-minute, one-skillet dinner that feels like a cozy Italian takeout night, and cherry tomatoes stay reliably flavorful year-round. Adapted from Ali Slagle's NYT Cooking recipe.

## Instructions

1. **Heat the broiler.** Position a rack about 6 inches from the heat source and heat the broiler.
2. **Sear the gnocchi.** In a large (12-inch) skillet over medium-high, heat enough olive oil to lightly coat the bottom of the pan (about 1 tablespoon). Add half the gnocchi, breaking up any that are stuck together. Cover with a lid or baking sheet and cook, undisturbed, until golden brown on one side, 2 to 4 minutes. Transfer to a medium bowl. Repeat with the remaining gnocchi and olive oil.
3. **Brown the butter and burst the tomatoes.** Add the butter to the skillet and cook over medium-high, stirring often, until golden-brown and toasty, 1 to 2 minutes. Add the garlic, red-pepper flakes, 1½ teaspoons salt and a few grinds of pepper, reducing the heat slightly if needed to avoid scorching. Add the tomatoes and 3 tablespoons water and cook, shaking the pan occasionally, until the tomatoes have softened and the liquid has slightly thickened, 4 to 6 minutes. Smash the tomatoes as they burst to help them along.
4. **Broil and serve.** Add the seared gnocchi and ¼ cup basil (if using), stir to coat, then shake into an even layer. Top with the mozzarella and drizzle lightly with olive oil. Broil until the cheese is melted and browned in spots, 2 to 4 minutes. Finish with more basil, red-pepper flakes and black pepper as desired.

## Notes

- **Storage:** Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days; they reheat well — and re-crisp — in a skillet over medium heat.
- **Commenter fixes:** NYT commenters (1,000+ of them) swear by two tweaks: sear the gnocchi uncovered while tossing constantly to avoid burning, and pull the pan off the heat while browning the butter so it and the sliced garlic don't scorch (return it to the burner once the residual heat fades).
- **Variations:** If your tomatoes taste more tart than sweet, stir in ½ teaspoon sugar in Step 3. Swap the water for chicken broth and add a handful of pine nuts and a light honey drizzle for extra depth (a reader favorite). Lazy night? Skip both pans — everything on a sheet pan at 220°C (about 425°F) for about 15 minutes, then add the mozzarella and broil.
- **Serving suggestion:** Serve straight from the skillet with crusty bread for the tomato-butter sauce, and a crisp green salad alongside.
