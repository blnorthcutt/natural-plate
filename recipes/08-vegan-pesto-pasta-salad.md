## Description

This bright green pasta salad wants an invite to your next picnic or desk lunch. Parmesan or pecorino are aged cheeses that typically contribute acidity, brininess and nuttiness to pesto, but here they're replaced by something equally brilliant: capers provide the salty tang, pistachios the crunch, and basil the punchy brightness. It's a delicious main course for a hot evening and keeps beautifully in the fridge.

## Instructions

1. **Cook the pasta.** Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the pasta and cook until tender. Drain, rinse under cold water until cool to the touch, then shake dry.
2. **Salt the tomatoes.** Meanwhile, transfer the tomatoes and a big pinch of salt to a large bowl. Stir to combine and set aside.
3. **Make the pesto.** Add the pistachios, capers, and garlic to a food processor and pulse until coarsely chopped. Add the basil and a pinch each of salt and pepper. Pulse until finely chopped. Add the lemon juice. With the motor running, gradually drizzle in the oil until well blended, scraping down the sides as needed. Scrape the pesto into the bowl of tomatoes.
4. **Combine.** Add the pasta to the pesto and tomatoes and stir until well coated. Let cool slightly, about 5 minutes, then stir in the arugula and a few torn basil leaves. Season to taste with salt.
5. **Serve or store.** The salad can sit for up to 2 hours, or refrigerated for up to 2 days. Refresh with salt, lemon juice, and oil as needed.

## Notes

- **Storage:** Refrigerated for up to 2 days. Pour some oil on top to keep the pesto from browning, and refresh with lemon and salt before serving.
- **Variations:** Use half the pasta and add a can of drained chickpeas for extra protein. Blanch the basil briefly to preserve its bright green color. Add olives or sautéed zucchini for more texture.
- **Serving suggestion:** Serve on a bed of extra arugula rather than mixing it in if you plan to store leftovers, so the greens don't get soggy.
