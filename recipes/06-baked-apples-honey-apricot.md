## Description

Baked apples are a humble dessert, but these have a certain elegance. Stuffed with dried apricots and golden raisins, glazed with honey and apricot jam, and served with a dollop of crème fraîche, they're delicious warm out of the oven or at room temperature. A perfect autumn or winter dessert that fills the kitchen with a warm, spiced fragrance.

## Instructions

1. **Heat the oven.** Heat the oven to 375°F/190°C.
2. **Prep the apples.** Peel the apples and remove the core with a small spoon or melon baller, keeping the apples intact. Melt 2 tablespoons of the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Brown the apples on all sides, then place them in a baking dish just large enough to hold them.
3. **Stuff the apples.** Spoon an equal amount of diced apricots and raisins into the cavity of each apple. Add 1 teaspoon honey, 1 teaspoon apricot jam, and 1 teaspoon butter to each apple. Drizzle the remaining honey evenly over the apples. Pour the wine or apple juice into the bottom of the dish.
4. **Bake.** Bake uncovered until the apples are golden and easily pierced with a fork, about 45 minutes. Every 10 minutes or so, baste the apples with pan juices.
5. **Reduce the juices.** Pour the remaining juices into a small saucepan and simmer over medium heat until reduced, about 7 minutes. Spoon the reduced juices over the apples. If desired, paint the apples with a little warmed apricot jam.
6. **Serve.** Serve warm or at room temperature. Just before serving, sprinkle with toasted flaked almonds and give each apple a dollop of crème fraîche.

## Notes

- **Storage:** Best served the day they're made, but leftovers keep in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat gently before serving.
- **Variations:** Swap the apricot jam for orange marmalade. Use maple syrup instead of honey. For a dairy-free version, use a plant-based butter alternative and coconut whipped cream.
- **Serving suggestion:** Great with vanilla ice cream or a spoonful of thick yogurt in place of crème fraîche.
