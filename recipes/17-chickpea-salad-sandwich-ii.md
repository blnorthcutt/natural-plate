## Description

The NYT Cooking take on the beloved chickpea salad sandwich: chickpeas mashed into a rich, creamy tahini-lemon dressing with grated Parmesan, celery, and scallions, then piled onto multigrain bread with crunchy lettuce, alfalfa sprouts, avocado, and tomato. It comes together in 10 minutes with zero cooking, sits beautifully for an hour or two, and tastes like a fancy deli lunch that happens to be mostly pantry staples. The heartier, more indulgent cousin of the classic chickpea salad sandwich already in this plan.

## Instructions

1. **Make the dressing.** In a large bowl, combine the olive oil, tahini, lemon juice, and grated garlic. Season with salt and pepper, then whisk until smooth and well combined.
2. **Mash the chickpeas.** Add the rinsed and drained chickpeas to the bowl and mash with the back of a fork or spoon until broken down but still chunky — you want texture, not paste.
3. **Mix the salad.** Add the finely chopped celery, scallions, and grated Parmesan. Mix well and season again with salt and pepper to taste.
4. **Assemble the sandwiches.** Spread a thin layer of mayonnaise on one side of each bread slice. On 4 of the slices, divide the lettuce, alfalfa sprouts, and avocado, then top with a generous scoop of the chickpea salad and the tomato slices. Close with the remaining bread and serve.

## Notes

- **Storage:** The chickpea salad keeps in the fridge for up to 5 days; assemble sandwiches fresh for the best texture. Assembled sandwiches can sit at room temperature for 1 to 2 hours before serving, making them great for picnics or packed lunches.
- **Variations:** For a tuna-salad-like texture, give the chickpeas a few churns in a food processor instead of mashing. Use vegan mayonnaise to keep it dairy-free, and swap the Parmesan for a vegan shred if you like. Many commenters love adding capers or a splash of red wine vinegar, and some prefer less tahini with more lemon — start with 4 tablespoons and adjust to taste.
- **Serving suggestion:** Serve with pickles, potato chips, or a handful of cherry tomatoes on the side.
