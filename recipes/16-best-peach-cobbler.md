## Description

A simple Southern cake-style cobbler that makes the most of ripe summer peaches — or the frozen ones hiding in the back of your freezer. All you need is a bowl, a saucepan, a baking pan and a spoon, plus about 20 minutes of hands-on time. The batter is deliberately sparse in the pan, so it rises up along the sides and through the peaches as it bakes, developing a crisp, golden exterior and a tender interior beneath the syrupy fruit. Adapted from the NYT Cooking classic, with reader-tested tweaks folded into the notes.

## Instructions

1. **Heat the oven.** Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. **Simmer the peaches.** Peel, pit and slice the peaches (skipping the peel is fine if you prefer — the skins soften as they bake). Melt 4 tablespoons of the butter in a medium saucepan. Add the sliced peaches and ½ cup of the sugar, stir in 2 tablespoons of the flour, and simmer for 10 to 15 minutes, until the peaches are soft and the liquid turns syrupy.
3. **Make the batter.** Meanwhile, in a medium bowl, combine the remaining 1 cup sugar with the remaining 1 cup flour, the baking powder and salt. Stir in the milk until just combined; the batter will be thick.
4. **Melt the pan butter.** Put the remaining 8 tablespoons butter in a 9-by-13-inch baking pan and place it in the oven until the butter melts. Carefully remove the hot pan. Reserve ⅓ cup of the batter, then add the rest to the pan, spreading it with a spatula as far as it will go — it will not fully cover the bottom, and that's O.K.
5. **Assemble.** Spoon the peach mixture evenly over the batter. Using a clean spoon, dollop small bits of the reserved batter over the peaches and spread them around a little with the back of the spoon. (It will not fully cover the peaches. Again, that's O.K.!)
6. **Bake.** Bake for about 1 hour, until the top is golden brown. Serve warm with vanilla ice cream, if desired.

## Notes

- **Storage:** This cobbler is best the day it's made — the crisp exterior softens when covered with plastic wrap or foil for storage. Cover loosely and reheat leftovers briefly in the oven or microwave.
- **Make-ahead:** Not a make-ahead dessert, but it comes together in minutes, and frozen peaches make it doable any time of year.
- **Variations:** NYT readers say the 9-by-13-inch pan is large for this amount of batter — double the batter for a taller cobbler with a higher cake-to-fruit ratio. To cut sweetness, reduce the sugar by ½ cup and the butter by 2 tablespoons. For classic Southern spice, stir in ½ teaspoon cinnamon and ¼ teaspoon nutmeg. The original recipe called for self-rising flour: if using it, skip the baking powder and use ¼ teaspoon salt instead of ¾. The cobbler also works in individual ramekins — just a dollop of peaches and a dollop of batter each.
- **Serving suggestion:** Serve warm with a scoop of vanilla ice cream; per the original recipe's note, it also makes a great breakfast.
