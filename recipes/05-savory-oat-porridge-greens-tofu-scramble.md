## Description

A warm, grounding breakfast that proves oats are not just for sweet. The creamy oat base is savory, creamy, and deeply savory from the miso, and the tofu scramble is a quick high-protein companion. This is a meal that sticks with you through a cold morning or a long day.

## Instructions

1. **Cook the oats.** In a small saucepan, combine the oats, water, and a pinch of salt. Bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer, stirring occasionally, for 5–7 minutes until thick and creamy. Stir in the miso paste and nutritional yeast until dissolved.
2. **Make the tofu scramble.** While the oats cook, crumble the tofu into a small bowl with your hands. Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat with the sesame oil. Add the diced onion and cook for 3 minutes. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute. Add the crumbled tofu, turmeric, a pinch of salt, and pepper. Cook for 4–5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tofu is heated through and lightly browned.
3. **Wilt the greens.** Add the kale to the tofu skillet. Cook for 2–3 minutes until wilted and bright green.
4. **Assemble.** Divide the savory oats between two bowls. Top with the tofu scramble and greens. Garnish with a drizzle of sesame oil or chili oil.

## Notes

- **Storage:** The oats keep for 4 days in the fridge. The tofu scramble for 3 days. Reheat separately and combine.
- **Variations:** Use spinach or Swiss chard instead of kale. Add a spoonful of sauerkraut or kimchi on the side for a probiotic boost. Top with toasted pumpkin seeds or hemp hearts.
- **Seasonal tip:** In spring, use asparagus and fresh herbs. In autumn, add roasted mushrooms with the onion.
