## Description

A fresh, vibrant, and bursting-with-flavor pasta salad that's perfect for picnics, potlucks, or easy lunches throughout the week. Tossed with a colorful mix of veggies, briny kalamata olives, and tangy feta, the real star is a zippy lemon-herb vinaigrette that brings everything together. Serve it cold or at room temperature — it's always a crowd favorite.

## Instructions

1. **Cook the pasta.** Cook the pasta al dente in a large stockpot of generously-salted water according to package instructions. Drain, then rinse under cold water for about 20–30 seconds until no longer hot. Transfer to a large mixing bowl.
2. **Make the vinaigrette.** Whisk together the olive oil, red wine vinegar, lemon juice, oregano, honey, minced garlic, black pepper, salt, and red pepper flakes until combined.
3. **Assemble the salad.** Add the cucumber, tomatoes, kalamata olives, feta cheese, and red onion to the mixing bowl. Drizzle all of the vinaigrette evenly on top and toss until everything is evenly coated.
4. **Rest and serve.** If you have time, let it rest in the fridge for 30 minutes so the flavors meld. Serve immediately, garnished with extra feta and black pepper if desired.

## Notes

- **Storage:** Keeps in the fridge for up to 5 days. It holds up well and actually gets better as it marinates.
- **Variations:** Add chickpeas or white beans for protein. Stir in baby spinach or arugula just before serving. Add artichoke hearts, roasted red peppers, or sun-dried tomatoes. Swap feta for goat cheese or shaved Parmesan.
- **Serving suggestion:** Excellent for meal prep — pack in individual containers for lunches throughout the week.
