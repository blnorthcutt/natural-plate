## Description

A lightning-fast dinner that tastes like a celebration. Warm corn tortillas piled with seasoned black beans, a bright lime-cabbage slaw, and creamy avocado. This is the meal that saves a busy weeknight — 25 minutes from start to table, and it requires nothing more than a quick chop and a hot pan.

## Instructions

1. **Make the slaw.** In a bowl, combine the shredded cabbage, the juice of 1 lime, a big pinch of salt, and most of the cilantro (save some for garnish). Toss and set aside. Let it sit while you prepare the beans — the acid softens the cabbage without wilting it.
2. **Season the beans.** Drain and rinse the black beans. Warm them in a small saucepan over medium heat with the cumin, chili powder, and a pinch of salt. Add a splash of water if they look dry. Keep warm.
3. **Warm the tortillas.** Heat a dry skillet or comal over medium-high heat. Warm each tortilla for about 30 seconds per side until pliable and lightly charred in spots. Wrap in a clean towel to keep warm.
4. **Assemble.** Lay 2 tortillas per person. Divide the black beans among them. Top with cabbage slaw, sliced avocado, and the remaining cilantro. Serve with lime wedges and optional salsa or hot sauce.

## Notes

- **Storage:** Best eaten fresh, but the components keep separately — beans for 5 days in the fridge, slaw for 2 days (it will soften).
- **Variations:** Add pickled red onions or quick-pickled jalapeños. Use pinto or refried beans instead of black. Add a dollop of plant-based yogurt or cashew crema.
- **Seasonal tip:** In summer, add diced tomato or grilled corn to the slaw. In winter, serve the beans over rice as a bowl instead.
