## Description

A quick, no-cook white bean hummus that swaps chickpeas for creamy cannellini beans, enriched with tahini and brightened with fresh lemon juice. Coriander seeds add a warm, aromatic note, and a touch of white miso (optional) gives the mellow beans a savory depth that makes this dip hard to stop eating. Ready in five minutes in a food processor, it's perfect for last-minute entertaining, meal-prep snacking, or packing for a picnic. Serve with warm pita, crudités, or spread on sandwiches.

## Instructions

1. **Pulse the garlic.** In a food processor, pulse the garlic clove until minced.
2. **Blend the dip.** Scrape down the bowl and add the rinsed cannellini beans, tahini, lemon juice, ground coriander, white miso (if using), and 1 teaspoon salt. Pulse until smooth, scraping down the bowl occasionally.
3. **Stream in the oil.** With the machine running, drizzle in the olive oil in a steady stream. The dip will emulsify and become light and creamy.
4. **Season and serve.** Season to taste with additional salt. Serve at room temperature or chilled, with a drizzle of olive oil and a sprinkle of coriander on top.

## Notes

- **Storage:** Keeps in the refrigerator in an airtight container for up to 1 week.
- **Make-ahead:** This dip actually improves after a day in the fridge as the flavors meld. Bring to room temperature and stir before serving.
- **Variations:** Double the lemon and add an extra drizzle of oil for a brighter, looser dip. Toast the coriander seeds before grinding for a deeper, nuttier flavor. Roast the garlic before adding for a mellower, sweeter allium note. A pinch of cumin or a splash of soy sauce (or liquid aminos) adds another layer of savoriness. Top with pomegranate seeds and pine nuts for a stunning presentation.
- **Serving suggestion:** Serve with warm pita, toasted flatbread, cucumber slices, radishes, cherry tomatoes, or crisp crudités. It also makes an excellent sandwich spread.
