## Description

A creamy, no-cook breakfast that comes together in 10 minutes the night before. The oats soften overnight into a pudding-like texture, and the chia seeds add a gentle thickening power along with omega-3s. Top with fresh berries and a drizzle of almond butter for a breakfast that keeps you steady until lunch.

## Instructions

1. **Mix the base.** In a bowl or jar, combine the rolled oats, chia seeds, plant milk, maple syrup, vanilla, and salt. Stir well.
2. **Refrigerate overnight.** Cover and refrigerate for at least 6 hours or overnight. The oats and chia will absorb the liquid and soften.
3. **Assemble in the morning.** Stir the oats — they should be thick and creamy. Add a splash of milk if too thick.
4. **Top and serve.** Divide between bowls, top with fresh berries and a drizzle of almond butter.

## Notes

- **Storage:** Keeps in the fridge for up to 4 days. Make a batch of 4 jars on Sunday for grab-and-go breakfasts.
- **Variations:** Swap berries for sliced banana and chopped apple in autumn. Use peanut butter instead of almond butter. Add 1 tbsp flax meal for extra fiber.
- **Seasonal tip:** In summer, use fresh local berries. In winter, use frozen berries (thaw them first) or grated apple with cinnamon.
