## Description

This Atlantic Beach Pie is a dreamy mash-up between a key lime and lemon meringue pie — a surprisingly fast and easy dessert adapted from Bill Smith, the longtime chef at Crook's Corner in Chapel Hill, N.C. A salty-sweet saltine cracker crust cradles a silky, tangy custard filling made with sweetened condensed milk and fresh citrus juice. Topped with billowy whipped cream, citrus zest, and a sprinkle of flaky sea salt, it's a lazy summer's day in pie form. No special equipment needed beyond a food processor (or your hands) and a pie pan.

## Instructions

1. **Heat the oven and make the crust.** Heat the oven to 350°F. In a food processor, pulse the saltine crackers until finely crushed but not quite dust — some little pieces are fine. (Alternatively, crush them by hand in a bowl.) Add the sugar and pulse to combine, then add the butter and pulse until the crumbs hold together like dough.

2. **Press, freeze, and bake the crust.** Press the mixture evenly into the bottom and up the sides of a 9-inch pie pan. Freeze for 15 minutes, then bake for 12 to 15 minutes, until the crust takes on a little color. Let cool slightly on a wire rack.

3. **Make the filling.** While the crust cools, in a medium bowl, whisk the egg yolks into the sweetened condensed milk until smooth. Whisk in the lemon or lime juice (or a mix of both) and the pinch of kosher salt, combining completely.

4. **Bake the pie.** Pour the filling into the warm crust and bake for 14 to 16 minutes, until the filling has set — it should jiggle slightly in the center when gently shaken but not be liquid. Transfer to a wire rack and let cool to room temperature, then refrigerate until completely cold, at least 4 hours or up to overnight.

5. **Serve.** Just before serving, whip the heavy cream to soft peaks (if using). Spread or dollop the whipped cream over the chilled pie, dust with citrus zest, and sprinkle with flaky sea salt. Slice and serve cold.

## Notes

- **Storage:** The baked and chilled pie (without toppings) keeps in the refrigerator for up to 2 days. Add whipped cream, zest, and salt only at serving time.
- **Make-ahead:** An ideal make-ahead dessert — bake and chill the pie a day ahead, then top with garnishes just before serving. The filling actually benefits from a full overnight chill.
- **Variations:** For a sturdier crust, some readers double the butter to 2 sticks and bake the crust for 30-35 minutes. A graham cracker or pretzel crust also works beautifully. Use all lemon juice, all lime juice, or a blend of both — a mix of lemons and limes gives the loveliest color and flavor. If you prefer a saltier crust, use salted butter or add an extra pinch of salt to the crumbs.
- **Serving suggestion:** Serve cold with a generous dollop of fresh whipped cream, a dusting of lemon or lime zest, and a sprinkle of flaky sea salt. The salt is essential to the Atlantic Beach Pie experience — it cuts through the sweetness and echoes the salty ocean breeze.
