## Description

For a vegan hot chocolate that rivals even the creamiest dairy-laden variety, add a few tablespoons of nut butter like almond, sunflower, or peanut to the nondairy milk, chocolate chips, and cocoa powder. The nut butter adds body and richness that makes this taste like a cozy café treat — ready in five minutes flat.

## Instructions

1. **Start the base.** Combine the chocolate chips, cocoa powder, nut butter, salt, and ½ cup of the nut or grain milk in a small saucepan.
2. **Whisk over medium heat.** Whisk over medium heat until the chocolate melts and the mixture is smooth, about 1½ minutes.
3. **Add the rest of the milk.** Whisk in 1 cup of nut or grain milk in a slow, steady stream, and continue whisking until the cocoa is steaming, about 1½ minutes. Don't let it come to a boil or the cocoa will separate.
4. **Taste and adjust.** If you prefer a thinner hot cocoa, add up to ½ cup additional milk. If you prefer it sweeter, add a teaspoon of sugar at a time, tasting after each addition.
5. **Serve.** Divide among cups and serve hot.

## Notes

- **Storage:** Best enjoyed immediately — but you can make it ahead and reheat gently, whisking to re-emulsify.
- **Variations:** A can of coconut milk makes it extra rich and dreamy. Add a pinch of cinnamon or cayenne for a spiced version. Use tahini instead of nut butter for a sesame twist.
- **Serving suggestion:** Top with vegan marshmallows, a dusting of cocoa, or a drizzle of melted chocolate.
